Han Solo (A New Hope) 1:6 Scale Collectible Figure

Straight from the dusty spaceports of Tatooine to the corridors of the Death Star, the Han Solo (A New Hope) Collectible Figure captures Star Wars’ favorite scoundrel at the start of his legendary journey. Dressed in his iconic black vest, v-neck shirt, and Corellian bloodstripe trousers, this release embodies the effortless charm, swagger, and quick-draw readiness of the Millennium Falcon’s captain.

Key Features

  • Authentic Character Likeness: Hand-painted head sculpt featuring a detailed likeness, realistic skin texture, and signature smirk.

  • Screen-Accurate Outfit & Rig: Tailored fabric vest, shirt, trousers with embroidered Corellian bloodstripes, and a detailed leather-like waist belt with working gun holster.

  • DL-44 Heavy Blaster Pistol: Die-cast metal DL-44 blaster with scope detailing, designed to fit cleanly in hand or slide securely into his low-slung holster.
